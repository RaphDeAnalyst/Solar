# 🌞 About the Website

This website is a **Nigerian-focused solar retail platform** built to help ordinary people make smarter decisions about solar.

The goal is simple:

* Educate Nigerians about solar power.
* Let them calculate what system they actually need.
* Show them solar bundles and products that match their needs.
* Make it easy to request a quote, talk to an expert, or buy products in the future.

Unlike other sellers who just throw random products at customers, this site guides them through the **real-life process of choosing solar**. It combines a **solar calculator**, an **AI-powered chatbot**, and a **product/bundle catalog** with price ranges to avoid confusion.

It’s designed for **speed and mobile-first experience** because most users in Nigeria access the web on smartphones with limited data.

For now, customers can’t pay online — instead they’ll request quotes and get follow-up via WhatsApp, email, or phone. Later, the site will expand into full e-commerce.

# ⚡ Real Case Scenario

Imagine **Chinedu**, a young professional living in Lagos. He’s tired of PHCN outages and his noisy generator.

1. **Discovery**

   * Chinedu Googles *“affordable solar system Nigeria”*.
   * He lands on the homepage.
   * He sees the hero banner: *“Power Your Home with Reliable Solar Solutions in Nigeria.”*
   * He notices the carousel of products and bundles, and the sticky mobile bottom bar: **Call | WhatsApp | Calculator | Chat**.

2. **Engagement**

   * Curious, Chinedu clicks **Run Calculator**.
   * He enters: TV (100W × 6h), Fan (70W × 10h), Lights (50W × 8h).
   * The calculator outputs:

     * Daily Load: 1,500 Wh
     * Inverter: 375W+
     * Battery: 1,700 Wh (140Ah @12V)
     * Panels: 2 × 400W
     * Charge Controller: 50A
     * Cost Estimate: ₦650,000 – ₦900,000

3. **Recommendation**

   * The calculator suggests: *Comfort Home Kit* (₦650k–₦900k).
   * It also says: “If you prefer plug-and-play, the **EcoFlow River 2 Pro** solar generator is an option.”

4. **Lead Capture**

   * Chinedu clicks *Request Quote*.
   * A form pops up, pre-filled with his calculator results.
   * He enters his name and WhatsApp number.

5. **Chatbot Support**

   * While waiting, he opens the chatbot.
   * He asks: *“Can this run a 1.5hp AC in Abuja for 10 hours?”*
   * The bot replies:

     * “You’ll need \~4,500 Wh/day. That means a 2kW inverter, 200Ah @48V battery, and 6 × 400W panels in Abuja. Recommended: **Premium Home Kit.**”

6. **Admin Follow-up**

   * In the Admin Dashboard, you (the owner) see Chinedu’s lead.
   * You click *Generate Quote* → PDF quote is emailed + WhatsApp’d to Chinedu.

7. **Conversion**

   * Chinedu is impressed by the professionalism.
   * He replies on WhatsApp: *“I’m ready, let’s go with the Comfort Home Kit.”*
